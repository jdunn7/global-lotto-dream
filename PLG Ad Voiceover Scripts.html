Voice & delivery guide

Tone Aspirational, premium, warm. Confident — never pushy, never "get-rich-quick." The dream is the product; sell the feeling, not the odds.
Pace Brand spots ~2 words/sec with room to breathe. Reels/TikTok faster and punchier. Let the jackpot number land.
Voice options A warm cinematic lead (M or F), plus a younger energetic read for social. Keep one consistent brand voice profile across the fleet.
Pronunciation "PlayLottoGlobal" (one word). EuroMillions · Mega‑Sena (MEH-ga SEH-na) · EuroJackpot · Naira (NY-ra).
Do Be hopeful, inclusive, global. Emphasize ease, security and "from anywhere." Always close on the responsible-play tag.
Don't Guarantee wins, promise income, imply certainty, or target minors / vulnerable players. No pressure tactics.
